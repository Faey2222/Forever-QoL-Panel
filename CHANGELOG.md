### Forever QoL Panel 1.5.0 – Action buttons and panel size

#### New
- **Action buttons:** icons are tinted when the target is out of range, you lack mana (rage, energy) or the spell cannot be used, and the remaining cooldown shows as a number on the button. Colours, font size, outline, shadow, the shortest cooldown with a number and tenths for short cooldowns can all be set, with a live preview in the options
- **Panel:** its height and the font size of its elements can be set, and how an element asks for attention (new mail): pulse, glow, blink or a pulsing icon

#### Fixes
- **Loot optimizer:** the talent tree is read from Forever's talent system, so your spec is rated correctly (it used the first tree before)
- **Loot optimizer:** gear better than a worn piece that is worth nothing for your spec is offered now
- **Loot optimizer:** spell school damage and resistances on items count
- **Bags full:** the window no longer opens empty; it updates with the loot and your bags and closes when there is nothing to offer
