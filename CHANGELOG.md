### Forever QoL Panel 1.1.1 – Ammo fix

A quick fix for hunters, thanks to your report.

#### Fixes
- **Ammo count:** the ammo element now shows how much ammo you carry. It counts the arrows or bullets in your bags and quiver – the kind your ranged weapon shoots – because WoW Forever does not report the ammo slot
- **Low ammo warning:** no more "No ammo" while your quiver is full; the warning uses the same count
- **Ammo element switched on:** it was off by default before and is now on once for everyone; only hunters see it, and you can turn it off again as usual

#### Options
- The options window shows the addon's version at the bottom
