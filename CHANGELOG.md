### Forever QoL Panel 1.8.1 – Completed quests in the group chat

#### New
- **Questing together – chat messages:** in a group the addon says in the group chat, with a link to the quest
  - when you complete a quest
  - as group leader: when everyone using the addon has completed a quest (with their names), when you pick a quest for the group and when you hide one
  - in the addon's language, with changing wording; each message can be switched off (Questing together > Chat)

#### Fixes
- **Loot optimizer:** the window for a better item in your bags only opens out of combat (armour cannot be changed in a fight); a fight that begins while it is open puts it aside until afterwards
