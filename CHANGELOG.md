### Forever QoL Panel 1.4.0 – Loot optimizer

#### New
- **Loot optimizer:** rates gear for your class and talent tree with the stat weights of [Pawn Forever](https://github.com/turlockmike/pawn-forever) (one set for levelling, one for the highest level; extra profiles like Shockadin or Battle Mage can be picked). Weights and hit cap can be changed per profile, and shared by export / import
  - **Tooltip:** a small tooltip below the item's shows how much better or worse it is than what you wear, with the stat differences
  - **Better loot:** a window offers to put on gear that is better, with the reasons and by how much; "Later" offers it again at a good moment (loading screen, level up, back in the bags)
  - **Bags full:** while looting, a window shows the cheapest item in your bags to swap for the loot, delete, skip or ignore
- **Quest items sparkle:** keeps the game's own sparkle on quest items in the world switched on, also after graphics presets (switching it on or off takes effect after a game restart)
- **Hunters:** the beast tooltip says whether your current pet can learn the ability, or from which pet level
- **Options:** the move mode button at the bottom left, the setup wizard and "What's new" on the General page; changes that need a reload ask when the window closes; the options window returns after the move mode
