### Forever QoL Panel 1.6.0 – Cast bars and warning sounds

#### New
- **Cast bars:** the cast bars below the target and focus frames (not the nameplates) can be shown as in the game, hidden, or placed freely in the move mode and scaled, which the standard interface cannot do
- **Warning sounds:** every kind of warning (class buffs, expiring buffs, food, flask, weapon buff, ammo) has its own sound to choose from 14 game sounds, played when that warning appears; the old single sound switch carries over
- **Move mode:** its window can be dragged anywhere (the place is kept) and has the panel's look, so nothing below it stays out of reach

#### Fixes
- **Bags full:** the loot row only looks at real items (no coins or currencies) and takes the name from the loot window when the item data is missing
