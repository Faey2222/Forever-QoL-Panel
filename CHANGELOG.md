### Forever QoL Panel 1.3.0 – Beast tooltip for hunters

#### New
- **Beast tooltip (hunters):** on a tameable beast you see which pet ability it teaches, with rank, marked "new" or "known", and its attack speed. Open Beast Training once so the addon knows your abilities. Ability names follow your client's language. Which beast teaches what comes from [Petopia](https://www.wow-petopia.com/forever/) – thank you!
- **More addons from us:** a new page at the end of the options with our other addons as tiles, with a CurseForge link to copy

#### Fixes
- **Options window:** the list on the left scrolls when the window is too low for it
