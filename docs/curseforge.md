# CurseForge texts

## Summary

An all-in-one quality-of-life addon for WoW Forever: info panel with XP and reputation bar, loot optimizer, smart action bars with range colours and cooldown numbers, quest markers, buff warnings, hunter beast info and more, in the look of the base game.

## Description

**Forever QoL Panel** bundles the quality-of-life features you would otherwise collect from a handful of addons into one package, built for World of Warcraft: Forever and designed to look like part of the base game.

Every feature can be switched on or off on its own. A short setup wizard walks you through the most useful settings on your first login, and after an update it shows only what is new.

### Features

**Info panel**
- A slim panel at the top or bottom of the screen that slides away when you don't need it
- Elements to choose from and arrange by drag and drop, sorted by theme: gold, session gold and XP, bag space, materials and ammo bags, durability, hearthstone (click to use), mailbox (glows on new mail), coordinates, zone, clock, stopwatch, latency, FPS, honor, PvP timer, soul shards for warlocks, ammo count for hunters and an addon menu
- Classic bronze look or a modern flat style, with your choice of border colour, width and corners
- Adjustable height and font size, and a choice of effects for elements that ask for attention (new mail)

**XP and reputation bar**
- XP bar with rested XP, tick marks and a preview of the XP your finished quests will give
- Optional status text: XP, percent, rested XP and time to level, on or below the bar
- Reputation bar for the faction you watch: attached to the XP bar, as its own bar below it or placed freely; at the highest level it takes the XP bar's place
- Both bars can stay on the panel or be placed anywhere on the screen

**Move mode with grid**
- One mode to place everything that can be moved: free bars, cast bars, warning groups and loot text
- Frames snap to an adjustable grid while you drag them

**Loot optimizer**
- Rates gear for your class and talent tree with the stat weights of Pawn Forever (one set for levelling, one for the highest level; extra profiles like Shockadin or Battle Mage)
- Item tooltips show how much better or worse a piece is than what you wear, with the stat differences
- Loot something better? A window offers to put it on and shows why and by how much; "Later" offers it again at a good moment
- Bags full while looting? A window shows the cheapest item in your bags to swap for the loot, delete, skip or ignore
- Weights and hit cap can be adjusted per profile and shared by export / import

**Quests and gameplay**
- Accept and turn in quests automatically (hold Shift to pause)
- Marks the most valuable quest reward and shows the sell value of each choice in coins
- Skips dialogues with a single option
- Sells grey items and repairs at merchants, guild funds first
- Faster auto loot and loot shown as floating text with icons
- Keeps the game's own sparkle on quest items in the world switched on, also after graphics presets

**Mail and characters**
- Pick your other characters on the realm as mail recipients with one click
- A list of all your characters, with the option to remove ones that no longer exist

**Action bars**
- Newly learned spells are placed on your bars automatically
- Higher ranks replace lower ones on every bar
- Icons tinted when the target is out of range, you lack mana or a spell cannot be used
- Cooldown numbers on the buttons, with your choice of colours, font size, outline and shadow, and a live preview in the options

**Cast bars**
- The cast bars below the target and focus frames (not on nameplates): as in the game, hidden, or placed freely and scaled

**Quest markers on nameplates**
- Units your quests still need get a kill or loot icon, optionally with the progress
- Position and size adjustable

**Buff and consumable warnings**
- Missing class buffs, food buff, flask or elixir, weapon buffs, buffs about to run out, and low ammo for hunters
- Choose where they apply: everywhere, dungeons and raids, or raids only; optionally quiet in cities and inns
- Movable, scalable, groupable, with optional animations
- Each kind of warning with its own sound, chosen from the game's sounds

**Tooltips**
- Sell value, including whole stacks
- How many of an item each of your characters owns, in bags and bank
- Required gathering skill on herbs, mining nodes and skinnable creatures, coloured by your own skill
- For hunters: which pet ability a tameable beast teaches, whether you know it already, whether your current pet can learn it, and the beast's attack speed

### Options

Open the options with **/fqp**, through the minimap button or by right-clicking the panel. The options window has a search field, so every setting is a few keystrokes away. **/fqp move** starts the move mode, **/fqp setup** opens the setup wizard again, and "What's new" on the General page shows what the latest version brought.
