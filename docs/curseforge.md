# CurseForge texts

## Summary

An all-in-one quality-of-life addon for WoW Forever: info panel with XP bar, smart action bars, quest markers, buff warnings, tooltips and more, in the look of the base game.

## Description

**Forever QoL Panel** bundles the quality-of-life features you would otherwise collect from a handful of addons into one package, built for World of Warcraft: Forever and designed to look like part of the base game.

Every feature can be switched on or off on its own, and a short setup wizard walks you through the most useful settings on your first login. After an update it shows only what is new.

### Features

**Info panel**
- A slim panel at the top or bottom of the screen with your XP bar built in
- XP bar with rested XP, tick marks and a preview of the XP your finished quests will give
- Elements to choose from and arrange by drag and drop: gold, session gold and XP, bag space, durability, hearthstone (click to use), coordinates, zone, clock, stopwatch, latency, FPS, honor, PvP timer and more
- Classic bronze look or a modern flat style, with your choice of border colour, width and corners

**Minimap**
- Collects the minimap buttons of other addons into one tidy menu, on the panel or on a floating button

**Action bars**
- Newly learned spells are placed on your bars automatically
- Higher ranks replace lower ones on every bar

**Quests and gameplay**
- Accept and turn in quests automatically (hold Shift to pause)
- Marks the most valuable quest reward and shows the sell value of each choice
- Skips dialogues with a single option
- Sells grey items and repairs at merchants, guild funds first
- Faster auto loot and loot shown as floating text

**Quest markers on nameplates**
- Units your quests still need get a kill or loot icon, optionally with the progress
- Position and size adjustable

**Buff and consumable warnings**
- Missing class buffs, food buff, flask or elixir, weapon buffs and buffs about to run out
- Choose where they apply: everywhere, dungeons and raids, or raids only
- Movable, scalable, groupable, with optional animations and sound

**Tooltips**
- Sell value, including whole stacks
- How many of an item each of your characters owns, in bags and bank
- Required gathering skill on herbs, mining nodes and skinnable creatures, coloured by your own skill

### Options

Open the options with **/fqp**, through the minimap button or by right-clicking the panel. The options window has a search field, so every setting is a few keystrokes away. **/fqp setup** opens the setup wizard again.

### Languages

English, German, French, Spanish, Russian and Simplified Chinese.
